Key Pattern Insights
Diurnal, monetary, and statistical findings
🌙
Late-Night Surge: Fraud peaks at 2:00 AM with a 1.71% rate — nearly 10× the baseline (0.173%) — when legitimate transaction volumes are at minimum.
💰
Dual-Price Tactic: Low-median ($9.25) probe transactions precede high-value fraud (max $2,125.87). Fraud mean ($122.21) exceeds legit mean ($88.29).
📉
Extreme Imbalance: Dataset ratio is 1 fraud per 578 legitimate transactions (0.173%), requiring SMOTE or class-weighted training.
💸
Financial Impact: Total fraud losses amount to $60,127.97, with a max single-transaction fraud of $2,125.87.

Strategic Recommendations
3 key actions from the analysis
🏗️ 1. Multi-Tiered Decision Engine
Deploy Random Forest as the primary automated classifier with 3-tier risk scoring (auto-approve / MFA challenge / freeze). Tune decision threshold based on fraud team review capacity.
🌙 2. Nighttime Dynamic Rules
Apply heightened sensitivity thresholds (2:00 AM – 4:00 AM) for cross-border and high-value card-not-present (CNP) transactions. Fraud rate is 10× baseline during this window.
🔄 3. Continuous Model Deployment
Implement monthly retraining pipelines. Monitor concept drift, especially for V17 and V14 which exhibit the strongest negative correlation with fraud labels.
Operational Trade-off: Catch Rate vs False Alarms
LR catches more fraud but at enormous false-alarm cost; RF offers the best balance
